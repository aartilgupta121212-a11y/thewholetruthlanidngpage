Got questions?

Let's dive in.

FAQ topics mirror the current protein-bar section on the official website. Answers below are kept intentionally conservative where the live page exposes the question but not the answer in crawlable text.

I don't go to the gym. Are protein bars for me?

The official site includes this question in its protein-bar FAQ. The practical use case is not limited to gym sessions: protein bars are positioned as convenient food/snacking options. Check the product nutrition and ingredients for the specific bar you choose.

How much protein is there in a bar?

The protein-bar catalogue includes a 20g filter and a dedicated Pro range. Exact nutrition should be checked on the individual product page for the specific SKU before purchase.

Is the protein veg?

The Pro bars shown on this page use whey protein concentrate, so they are milk-derived. The product ingredient list is the right source for each SKU.

I'm lactose intolerant. Is whey okay for me?

Whey is milk-derived. If you have lactose intolerance or another dietary concern, use the ingredient and nutrition information on the specific product page and make the choice appropriate to your needs.

Are any of these bars vegan?

The Pro bars featured here contain whey protein concentrate, so they are not vegan. Always check the individual product ingredients for other ranges.

Do the bars have sugar?

Several current product pages state “no added refined sugar” and show dates as an ingredient. That is different from saying the bar contains no naturally occurring sugars. See the full nutrition panel for the exact SKU.

Who makes these bars I’m buying?

The Whole Truth Foods publishes manufacturing details and FSSAI licence information on its official manufacturing page. The protein-bar category also links shoppers to the Bada Barkhana story.
